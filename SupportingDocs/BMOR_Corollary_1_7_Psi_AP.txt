BMOR (Bennett–Martin–O’Bryant–Rechnitzer, 2018) — explicit PNT in AP for ψ

Citation (as given by our consulted mathematician):
  Bennett–Martin–O’Bryant–Rechnitzer (2018), Corollary 1.7.

Statement (ψ in arithmetic progressions, explicit and unconditional):
  For integers q,a with 1 ≤ q ≤ 10^5 and gcd(a,q)=1, and for all real x ≥ 10^6,
    | ψ(x; q, a) - x / φ(q) | < 0.011 * x / log x.

Notes for this repo:
  - Our major-arc truncation uses Q0 = 30000, so q ≤ Q0 is covered since 30000 ≤ 10^5.
  - The project’s ψ is the Chebyshev ψ-function with von Mangoldt weights:
      ψ(x; q, a) = ∑_{n ≤ x, n ≡ a (mod q)} Λ(n).
  - The RHS is division by log x (not multiplication).
